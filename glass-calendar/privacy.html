DAYLIGHT / LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026. This policy describes the current Daylight Calendar browser application, hosted on GitHub Pages. Daylight is a locally stored calendar and tasks application, not an encrypted cloud account service.

Information you enter

Daylight lets you enter your display name, events, event notes, locations, tasks, calendar names, preferences and calendar subscription URLs. These are generally stored in your browser's local storage, on the device and browser where you use the site. A different device or browser will not automatically receive them. Export your calendar to preserve a copy before clearing browser data.

Third-party address search

If you type three or more characters in the location field, Daylight may send your search text to the public Photon geocoding service at photon.komoot.io to offer address suggestions based on OpenStreetMap data. Your IP address and request metadata may be processed by that service. Do not enter sensitive personal information into address search. Suggestions are optional and manual entry remains available. See the OpenStreetMap attribution.

Calendar links and imports

Imported iCalendar files and saved subscription URLs can include personal event information. Daylight stores this data in your browser. On static GitHub Pages hosting, the server-side feed proxy is not running, so remote calendar subscription refresh may be unavailable. Please avoid providing private subscription URLs to third parties.

Hosting and external services

GitHub Pages delivers the website and may collect technical connection and access logs under GitHub's own policies. The Inter font is loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive network request metadata. GitHub, Google and the optional address-search provider have their own privacy practices. Daylight does not currently provide user accounts, automatic cloud synchronization, or end-to-end encryption.

Notifications

Browser reminders are optional and require your device's permission. They work only while the app is running; they are not a background push-notification service.

Storage and deletion

To delete Daylight data, use your browser's site-data controls for this website. Clearing browser storage can permanently delete locally saved entries. A downloaded export remains under your own control.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as Daylight adds features. Changes will be published here with an updated effective date. For questions, issues or requests, use the project's GitHub issues page. Please do not post private personal information publicly.
